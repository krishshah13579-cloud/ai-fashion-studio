## 🚀 How to Run (100% Free Forever)

Because high-fashion virtual try-on requires high-end GPU power (12–16GB VRAM), we run the heavy AI engine on **Google's Free Cloud T4 GPU** (Google Colab), completely free of cost.

### Step 1: Start Your Private Free AI Engine (Colab)
1. Go to [Google Colab](https://colab.research.google.com).
2. Click **File** > **Upload Notebook** and select:
   `ai-fashion-studio/fashion_ai_studio_colab.ipynb`
3. In the top menu, go to **Runtime** > **Change runtime type** > Select **T4 GPU** (Free).
4. Click **Runtime** > **Run all**.
5. At the bottom of the notebook, you will see a public live link:
   `Running on public URL: https://xxxx.gradio.live`
   *(Copy this URL)*

### Step 2: Use the Studio Web App
1. Open [`index.html`](./index.html) in any web browser (Safari / Chrome).
2. Paste your `https://xxxx.gradio.live` link into the **Free GPU Engine URL** box and click **Connect**.
3. Select your Zara/H&M base model.
4. Upload your product photo (flat-lay or mannequin).
5. Click **Generate Zara Model Photo** $\rightarrow$ In 15–20 seconds, your photo is ready to download!

## 📸 3 Secrets to Get the "Zara / H&M" Look from Phone Photos

1. **Iron / Steam First:** The AI preserves fabric texture accurately. If your t-shirt has fold lines, the model photo will show them.
2. **Even Daylight:** Shoot your flat-lay near an open window during daytime. Avoid yellow ceiling bulbs or heavy phone flash.
3. **Square & Straight Angle:** Keep your phone parallel to the clothing when taking the photo.
